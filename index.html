Our Heritage, Our Strength

BAUSDA was founded to unite students of Bafumbira descent across all Ugandan universities. We are a home away from home, where our culture thrives, our language is spoken, and our traditions are celebrated. Whether you're from Kisoro, Bufumbira, Kanaba, Muramba, or any part of our beautiful land, you belong here.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our community

Obumwe

Unity - We stand together as one people, regardless of which university we attend or which county we come from.

Icyubahiro

Cultural Pride - We preserve and celebrate our rich Bafumbira heritage, language, and traditions.

Uburezi

Education - We support each other to achieve academic excellence in our various institutions.

Kwihangana

Perseverance - We encourage each other to overcome challenges and succeed in our endeavors.
