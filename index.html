Our Heritage, Our Strength

The Ankole Students' Association was founded to unite students of Ankole descent across all Ugandan universities. We are a home away from home, where our culture thrives, our language is spoken, and our traditions are celebrated. Whether you're from Mbarara, Bushenyi, Ntungamo, Kiruhura, Ibanda, or any part of Ankole land, you belong here.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our community

Obusingye

Unity - We stand together as one people, regardless of which university we attend or which district we come from.

Eby'engoma

Cultural Pride - We preserve and celebrate our rich Ankole heritage, language, and traditions.

Amashomo

Education - We support each other to achieve academic excellence in our various institutions.

Kugumisiriza

Perseverance - We encourage each other to overcome challenges and succeed in our endeavors.
