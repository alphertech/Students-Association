Our Story

Founded in 1995 by a group of visionary Ankole students at Makerere University, our association began as a small gathering of 20 students who wanted to maintain their cultural connections while pursuing higher education far from home.

Over the past 29 years, we've grown into a vibrant community of over 500 members across 12+ universities in Uganda. What started as informal meetings in dormitories has evolved into a fully-fledged national organization with executive leadership, university chapters, and a strong alumni network.

Today, we continue our founders' mission: to ensure no Ankole student feels alone in their academic journey, to preserve our rich cultural heritage, and to build a network that supports both students and citizens of Ankole.

Our Mission

To unite Ankole students across Ugandan universities, preserve and celebrate our rich cultural heritage, and provide a comprehensive support network that fosters academic excellence and personal growth.

Our Vision

A future where every Ankole student thrives academically, remains deeply connected to their roots, and emerges as a leader who contributes to both their community and Uganda at large.

Our Core Values

01

Obusingye (Unity)

We believe that together we are stronger. Regardless of which university we attend or which district we come from, we stand as one Ankole family, supporting each other through every challenge and celebrating every victory together.

02

Eby'engoma (Cultural Pride)

We honor and actively preserve our Ankole heritage. Through Ekitaguriro dance performances, Runyankole language workshops, traditional ceremonies, and storytelling, we ensure our culture thrives for generations to come.

03

Amashomo (Education)

Education is the key to our future. We maintain high academic standards through peer tutoring, study groups, and mentorship programs. We celebrate academic achievements and support those who struggle.

04

Kugumisiriza (Perseverance)

The journey through university can be challenging, especially far from home. We encourage each other to persevere, overcome obstacles, and emerge stronger. No one faces difficulties alone.

05

Kugabira (Giving Back)

We believe in lifting others as we climb. Our members are encouraged to mentor younger students, contribute to community development, and give back to the Ankole region through various initiatives.

06

Obusinge (Respect)

We treat all members with dignity and respect. We honor our elders, support our peers, and welcome everyone who wishes to learn about and contribute to Ankole culture.
