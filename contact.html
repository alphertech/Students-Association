FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the association?

You can join by visiting the university organisation from any university in uganda where we do exist.

I'm not from Bufumbira. Can I still join?

While our focus is on Bafumbira culture and community, we welcome anyone who respects our heritage and wants to learn. We have several associate members from other regions who actively participate in our events.

How can I start a chapter at my university?

If your university doesn't have a chapter yet, contact our National Secretary at secretary@bausda.ug. We'll guide you through the process of establishing a chapter, including recruiting founding members and holding elections.

What events do you organize?

We organize cultural events (Ikibwino festivals, Rufumbira language workshops), academic events (study groups, career fairs), social gatherings, and leadership retreats. Check our Events page for upcoming activities.

Can parents or citizens attend events?

Absolutely! Our events are open to all Bafumbira citizens. We especially welcome parents and elders who can share wisdom and mentor our youth. Many events are specifically designed for intergenerational connection.

How do I become a mentor?

If you're an alumnus or professional interested in mentoring. We'll match you with students based on your profession and interests.
