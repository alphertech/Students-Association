FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the association?

You can join by visiting our Join page and filling out the membership form. Student membership is UGX 10,000 per year, and citizen membership is UGX 25,000 per year. You'll receive a welcome email within 48 hours.

I'm not from Ankole. Can I still join?

While our focus is on Ankole culture and community, we welcome anyone who respects our heritage and wants to learn. We have several associate members from other regions who actively participate in our events.

How can I start a chapter at my university?

If your university doesn't have a chapter yet, contact our National Secretary at secretary@ankolestudents.ug. We'll guide you through the process of establishing a chapter, including recruiting founding members and holding elections.

What events do you organize?

We organize cultural events (Ekitaguriro festivals, Runyankole language workshops), academic events (study groups, career fairs), social gatherings, and leadership retreats. Check our Events page for upcoming activities.

Can parents or citizens attend events?

Absolutely! Our events are open to all Ankole citizens. We especially welcome parents and elders who can share wisdom and mentor our youth. Many events are specifically designed for intergenerational connection.

How do I become a mentor?

If you're an alumnus or professional interested in mentoring, please fill out the mentor application form on our Resources page. We'll match you with students based on your profession and interests.
