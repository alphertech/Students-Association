Tax Information

The Ankole Students' Association is a registered Non-Governmental Organization in Uganda (Reg. No. 12345). Donations are tax-deductible for Ugandan taxpayers. International donors will receive a receipt for their records. For corporate partnerships or large donations, please contact our finance team at finance@ankolestudents.ug.
